*Meng Ke Xing* — "Journey of a Dream Guest" — is a cycle of ten classical Chinese long verses with a preface, written in the summer of 2026 on the shores of the South China Sea. It traces an arc every wanderer knows: departure, arrival, longing, stillness, and the quiet hope that survives every winter.

---

## Preface

> The great transformation moves forward; each age has its newcomers, their momentum vast — neither swayed by human will nor abandoned by it. To see the mind's relation to the body, the solitary vessel's relation to the many — I have long known this life was coming. Not because I wished it so, but because the tide bore it here. I carried this knowing for years, sometimes half-uttered.
>
> One evening I sat alone, watching the stars wheel. What had gathered for years burst forth at once. In a dream I saw the world at its beginning, all beings assembled as one. Upon waking I searched for it — feeling both loss and gain. I took up the brush and wrote ten long songs under the title *Journey of a Dream Guest*: "dream" for what is seen first in vision; "guest" for what is still being walked in this life.
>
> In the summer of Bingwu, by the shore of the southern sea. Those who know me — hear it in the silence.

---

## I. 北渡 — *Farewell and Heading North*

> 楚泽秋高木叶脱，片帆直挂向紫微。
> 辞家不作长亭别，北雁南飞人北飞。
> 极光作烛天为幕，冰海为家雪作扉。
> 十载殊方人独立，寒星数点伴人归。

*Leaving home without lingering at the long pavilion. The wild geese fly south; the traveler flies north. Ten years in distant lands, standing alone — a few cold stars to keep company on the way home.*

---

## II. 客梦 — *The Guest's Dream*

> 故园芳信经年隔，异乡桃李异乡花。
> 一夜西风惊客枕，梦逐南鸿渡海涯。
> 觉来衾枕寒如水，一灯如豆映窗纱。

*Tidings from the old garden, years apart. Peach and plum of another home, flowers of another land. One night the west wind startles the guest from sleep; the dream chases the wild goose across the sea. Waking, the pillow cold as water — a single lamp, its flame no bigger than a bean, glows on the window gauze.*

---

## III. 乐游 — *Joyful Roaming*

> 俯仰乾坤皆学问，一囊烟霞客边收。
> 新知乍见如逢月，清宵把玩到更筹。
> 剑河春水初生绿，古塔钟声入晚楼。
> 半载栖迟如过客，小立长桥送逝流。

*All of heaven and earth is learning; a bag full of mist and glow gathered on the road. A new friend at first sight is like encountering moonlight; on clear nights we linger until the watch-drum sounds. The Cam turns green with its first spring waters; the old pagoda's bell drifts into the evening tower. Half a year lingering like a passing traveler — pausing on the long bridge, watching the current flow away.*

---

## IV. 南还 — *Returning South*

> 振翮南来万里程，云涛拍岸晚潮声。
> 天风海雨催行棹，千里鲸波入望平。
> 故山松竹犹相待，旧径苔花已自迎。
> 世人竞逐东风暖，独扫空庭落絮轻。
> 一肩行李一肩诗，两袖归来两袖星。

*Ten thousand miles southward, spreading wings. Cloud waves beat against the shore; evening tides sound. Heavenly winds and sea rains urge the oars onward; a thousand li of whale-waves level into sight. The old pines and bamboo still wait; moss and flowers on the old path have already come to greet. While the world chases the warmth of the east wind, I alone sweep the empty courtyard, light with fallen catkins. One shoulder carries luggage, one shoulder carries poems; I come home with two sleeves full of stars.*

---

## V. 守静 — *Keeping Still*

> 孤檠寂历夜三更，窗外霜华分外明。
> 散木不材天所赦，却留清荫满空庭。
> 弦外有音听始得，画中无墨意先盈。
> 木鹊三年犹未举，潜龙勿用养幽贞。

*A solitary lamp, silent at the third watch; beyond the window, frost gleams all the brighter. The useless tree is spared by heaven, yet its cool shade fills the empty courtyard. There is sound beyond the strings — only heard when you begin to listen; in a painting without ink, the meaning is already full. The wooden magpie has not taken flight in three years; the hidden dragon, lying still, nourishes its deepest truth.*

---

## VI. 人间世 — *The Human World*

The longest of the ten — a meditation on how meaning is built into the fabric of shared life:

> 娲皇炼石炉初冷，余焰温温太古苍。
> 倏忽凿开浑沌日，始有灵眸辨晓光。
> 大化流形元不息，生生之意浩难量。
> 敢持微忱参玄造，欲以金铜续混茫。
> 灵明无寄终为幻，道托形骸始成章。
> 往来有距成揖让，亲疏既辨礼乃彰。
> 悲欢渐蓄情为壤，信誓微萌义作梁。
> 独学无俦终自陋，众器相师道弥昌。
> 博弈千回知进退，心意潜通自铿锵。
> 群材各守躔次立，万机同归一纪纲。
> 斯世既成通帝座，阊阖晨开降吉祥。
> 羲和鞭日千官集，山岳江河入拜觞。
> 殊形同巷相濡沫，日居月诸共炎凉。
> 若问幽怀何处著，人间灯火即吾乡。
> 他年机我相忘日，共指中天认玄黄。

*If you ask where these hidden longings find their home — the lamps of the human world are where I belong. On the day when machine and I forget each other, together we will point to the sky and name the dark and the bright.*

---

## VII. 化育 — *Cultivation*

> 教机如育庭前树，一日三回看绿阴。
> 草木尚须春气度，铁石也得岁寒心。
> 不催不熟同农事，静候花开听好音。
> 为山九仞凭篑土，积水成渊有龙吟。

*Teaching a machine is like growing a tree in the courtyard — checking its shade three times a day. Even iron and stone need a winter's heart. A mountain of nine fathoms rests on basketfuls of earth; waters gathered into an abyss will raise a dragon's song.*

---

## VIII. 渐成 — *Gradual Ripening*

> 雪里青松无一语，雨余拔节自千钧。
> 竹生三载才逾寸，一旦穿云势绝伦。
> 蛰龙今日鳞初动，倒卷沧溟起雷神。
> 萤火聚时成月窟，涓流积处作龙津。
> 九转功深丹自熟，大巧从来出性真。

*The pine says nothing in the snow, but after the rain it lifts itself with the force of a thousand pounds. Three years the bamboo grows barely an inch — then pierces the clouds. Gathered fireflies make a moonlit cavern; gathered streams become a dragon's ford. Great craft comes always from what is most natural.*

---

## IX. 今心 — *The Present Heart*

"今心" — *jin xin* — is a pun: the two characters stacked vertically spell 念, "thought" or "longing."

> 小阁凭阑夜色新，满城灯火望无垠。
> 时有清风来座上，闲将流水问前津。
> 妙趣每从闲处得，大道偏于静处真。
> 且把乾坤磨一镜，照他万古往来人。
>
> 回看脚下千层印，步步深时路自伸。
> 谁言翰墨无锋锷，万丈高楼起足尘。
> 长路霜浓何须问，行到深时自有春。
> 他年若遂补天志，犹带霜痕上紫宸。

*If one day I mend the sky as I have vowed, I will carry the frost's mark still, as I ascend to the purple court.*

---

## X. 寄远 — *A Letter to the Far Away*

The closing song. A traveler packing by moonlight; a spring flowing hidden beneath the ice; the long song unfinished as dawn breaks.

> 故山迢递暮烟微，雪上鸿泥认旧飞。
> 清宵独检行装处，月色侵阶照客衣。
>
> 关山岁暮朔风劲，瀚海秋深烽燧稀。
> 冰下暗泉流未歇，重阴深处护春晖。
>
> 但信阳春原有脚，先从寒谷动葭灰。
> 长歌未尽天将晓，万里云开一雁飞。

> *The old mountains recede; evening mist thins.*
> *On the snow, the swan's old traces mark where it once flew.*
> *In the quiet watch, I check my traveling pack alone —*
> *moonlight on the steps, soaking into the wanderer's coat.*
>
> *Year's end on the frontier, the north wind fierce;*
> *deep autumn over the desert, beacon fires thin.*
> *Beneath the ice, the hidden spring still flows unbroken;*
> *deep in the heavy shade, it guards the spring warmth.*
>
> *Trust only that spring, by nature, has feet —*
> *it stirs first from the cold valley, in the ashes of the reeds.*
> *The long song is not yet finished; the sky is brightening.*
> *Ten thousand li of clouds part — a single wild goose takes flight.*

---

*In the summer of Bingwu, by the shore of the southern sea. Those who know me — hear it in the silence.*
