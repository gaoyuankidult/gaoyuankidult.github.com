*Meng Ke Xing* — "Journey of a Dream Guest" — is a cycle of ten classical Chinese long verses with a preface, written in the summer of 2026 on the shores of the South China Sea. It traces an arc every wanderer knows: departure, arrival, longing, stillness, and the quiet hope that survives every winter.

I'm sharing the full typeset pages here, because the typography is part of the poem.

---

## The Preface

> The great transformation moves forward; each age has its newcomers, their momentum vast — neither swayed by human will nor abandoned by it. To see the mind's relation to the body, the solitary vessel's relation to the many — I have long known this life was coming. Not because I wished it so, but because the tide bore it here. I carried this knowing for years, sometimes half-uttered.
>
> One evening I sat alone, watching the stars wheel. What had gathered for years burst forth at once. In a dream I saw the world at its beginning, all beings assembled as one. Upon waking I searched for it — feeling both loss and gain. I took up the brush and wrote ten long songs under the title *Journey of a Dream Guest*: "dream" for what is seen first in vision; "guest" for what is still being walked in this life.
>
> In the summer of Bingwu, by the shore of the southern sea. Those who know me — hear it in the silence.

## 其六 · 人间世 — *The Human World*

The longest of the ten, a meditation on how meaning is built into the fabric of shared life:

> 娲皇炼石炉初冷，余焰温温太古苍。
> 倏忽凿开浑沌日，始有灵眸辨晓光。
> 大化流形元不息，生生之意浩难量。
> 敢持微忱参玄造，欲以金铜续混茫。
> 灵明无寄终为幻，道托形骸始成章。
> 往来有距成揖让，亲疏既辨礼乃彰。
> 悲欢渐蓄情为壤，信誓微萌义作梁。
> 独学无俦终自陋，众器相师道弥昌。
> 博弈千回知进退，心意潜通自铿锵。
> 群材各守躔次立，万机同归一纪纲。
> 斯世既成通帝座，阊阖晨开降吉祥。
> 羲和鞭日千官集，山岳江河入拜觞。
> 殊形同巷相濡沫，日居月诸共炎凉。
> 若问幽怀何处著，人间灯火即吾乡。
> 他年机我相忘日，共指中天认玄黄。

*The last couplet: "If you ask where these hidden longings find their home — the lamps of the human world are where I belong. On the day when machine and I forget each other, together we will point to the sky and name the dark and the bright."*

## 其九 · 今心 — *The Present Heart*

"今心" — *jin xin* — is a pun: the two characters stacked vertically spell 念, "thought" or "longing."

> 小阁凭阑夜色新，满城灯火望无垠。
> 时有清风来座上，闲将流水问前津。
> 妙趣每从闲处得，大道偏于静处真。
> 且把乾坤磨一镜，照他万古往来人。
>
> 回看脚下千层印，步步深时路自伸。
> 谁言翰墨无锋锷，万丈高楼起足尘。
> 长路霜浓何须问，行到深时自有春。
> 他年若遂补天志，犹带霜痕上紫宸。

*The last couplet: "If one day I mend the sky as I have vowed, I will carry the frost's mark still, as I ascend to the purple court."*

## 其十 · 寄远 — *A Letter to the Far Away*

The closing song. A traveler packing by moonlight; a spring flowing hidden beneath the ice; the long song unfinished as dawn breaks.

> 故山迢递暮烟微，雪上鸿泥认旧飞。
> 清宵独检行装处，月色侵阶照客衣。
>
> 关山岁暮朔风劲，瀚海秋深烽燧稀。
> 冰下暗泉流未歇，重阴深处护春晖。
>
> 但信阳春原有脚，先从寒谷动葭灰。
> 长歌未尽天将晓，万里云开一雁飞。

> *The old mountains recede; evening mist thins.*
> *On the snow, the swan's old traces mark where it once flew.*
> *In the quiet watch, I check my traveling pack alone —*
> *moonlight on the steps, soaking into the wanderer's coat.*
>
> *Year's end on the frontier, the north wind fierce;*
> *deep autumn over the desert, beacon fires thin.*
> *Beneath the ice, the hidden spring still flows unbroken;*
> *deep in the heavy shade, it guards the spring warmth.*
>
> *Trust only that spring, by nature, has feet —*
> *it stirs first from the cold valley, in the ashes of the reeds.*
> *The long song is not yet finished; the sky is brightening.*
> *Ten thousand li of clouds part — a single wild goose takes flight.*
